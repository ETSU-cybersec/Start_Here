# What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data from attacks, unauthorized access, and other security threats. It combines technology, problem-solving, and critical thinking to understand how systems work, how they can be attacked, and how they can be protected.

Cybersecurity is a broad field with many different areas, including:

- Penetration Testing and Ethical Hacking
- Network and System Security
- Digital Forensics and Incident Response
- Security Operations and Threat Analysis
- Malware Analysis and Reverse Engineering
- Governance, Risk, and Compliance

You do not need to know which area interests you yet. Part of learning cybersecurity is exploring these different areas and finding what you enjoy.

## What We Do

Cyber Bucs gives students a place to learn, practice, and explore cybersecurity outside of the classroom. Our focus is on gaining practical experience, working together, and helping members develop their technical skills.

Throughout the club, you may participate in hands-on labs, Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, cybersecurity competitions, workshops, projects, and technical demonstrations. Members can also share resources, help each other solve problems, and prepare for certifications and careers in cybersecurity.

## What You Can Expect to Learn

As you participate in Cyber Bucs, you will have opportunities to build experience with:

- Linux and the command line
- Networking and common protocols
- Cybersecurity tools and techniques
- Offensive and defensive security
- CTF challenges and competitions
- Working with and learning from a team

You will also learn how to approach technical problems, research solutions, document your work, and continue learning independently.

## Do I Need Experience?

No previous cybersecurity experience is required.

Cyber Bucs welcomes students at all skill levels. Whether you are opening a Linux terminal for the first time or already have experience with cybersecurity tools and competitions, there are opportunities to learn, practice, and contribute.

Cybersecurity is a large field, and nobody starts out knowing everything. Ask questions, try new things, work with other members, and find the areas of cybersecurity that interest you.
